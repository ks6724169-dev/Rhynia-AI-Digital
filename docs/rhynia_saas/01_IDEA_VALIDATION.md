## 1. Idea Statement

**"Rhynia Intelligence — ek AI chat SaaS platform jo Bharatiya chatro aur aam logon ko
shuddh Hindi/English me shisht, satik aur imandaar jawab deta hai. Model: apna khud ka
fine-tuned Rhynia (Qwen3-VL-8B base), apna platform — kisi third-party AI par nirbhar nahi."**

## 2. Problem

| Samasya | Tark |
|---|---|
| Bharatiya chatro ko apni bhasha me quality AI nahi milta | ChatGPT jaise tools English-first; Hinglish par desi tone nahi |
| Data privacy ki chinta | Log nahi chahte har baat American server par jaye |
| Identity confusion | User puche "kaun ho tu?" to jawab saaf ho — Rhynia, ChatGPT nahi |

## 3. Target Users (pehle 1,000 users)

1. **Chatr (primary)** — padhai, notes, exam preparation (Tier-2/3 cities focus)
2. **Developers** — coding help (Module C, baad me)
3. **Aam users** — samanya gyan, rozmarra ke sawal

## 4. Unique Value Proposition

- **Bhasha Mirror Rule** — Hinglish me pucho, shuddh Devanagari me jawab
- **Zero-Leakage Identity** — hamesha Rhynia, kabhi ChatGPT/Qwen nahi
- **Education-first** — notes, exam help (Module A = asli product)
